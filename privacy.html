This policy explains how we handle your personal data when you order from SILA or contact us. It is written under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer — we are not required to.

2. What we collect

Only what we need to make and deliver your ring:

  • your name;
  • your phone number / WhatsApp number;
  • your email address;
  • your delivery address in Bali;
  • your ring size;
  • your order details and payment confirmation;
  • the content of the messages you send us.

We do not ask for, and do not want, any special category data (health, beliefs, and so on).

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

What forLegal basis
Taking, making and delivering your order; handling exchanges and returnsPerformance of a contract with you — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR

We do not send marketing emails or marketing messages, and we do not profile you.

4. Who else sees your data

We share your data only with those who need it to deliver your order:

  • the courier company that delivers your ring in Bali — name, address, phone number;
  • payment providers — card payment provider, PayPal, Wise, and our bank, depending on how you pay;
  • our website hosting and domain provider;
  • our email provider;
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, if you contact us through WhatsApp or Instagram — your message and profile data are processed by Meta under its own terms, and we cannot control that;
  • our accountant in Poland;
  • public authorities, where the law requires it.

Everyone processing data on our behalf does so under a written data processing agreement.

5. Data leaving the European Economic Area

Your ring is delivered in Indonesia, and Indonesia is not covered by an EU adequacy decision. To deliver your order we pass your name, address and phone number to a courier company in Bali. This transfer is necessary for the performance of your contract with us, which is the basis permitted under Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR.

Some of our payment and messaging providers are based in, or transfer data to, the United States. Where they do, they rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

6. How long we keep it

  • Invoices and accounting records: 5 years from the end of the tax year in which the invoice was issued — this is a legal requirement we cannot shorten.
  • Order and delivery data: for the period in which claims may still be brought, then deleted.
  • Messages that did not lead to an order: 12 months.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you access to the data we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data, where we are not required to keep it;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • hand it over to you or to someone else in a portable format;
  • stop using it, where we rely on legitimate interest — you may object at any time.

Write to sila.thejewelry@gmail.com and we will respond within one month.

If you think we are handling your data badly, you can complain to the Polish supervisory authority:

Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO)
ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland
uodo.gov.pl

You may also complain to the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live.

8. Cookies

Our website uses only the technical cookies needed for it to work. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not build profiles of visitors. This is why you do not see a cookie banner.

If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

9. Is providing data mandatory?

Giving us your data is voluntary — but without your name, contact details, address and ring size we cannot make or deliver your order.

10. Automated decisions

We do not make any decisions about you by automated means, and we do not carry out profiling.

11. Security

We keep your data on password-protected accounts with access limited to the people who need it. We do not store full card numbers at any point — card payments are handled entirely by our payment provider.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page, with the date it was last changed.
