2. What we collect

Only what we need to make and deliver your ring:

  • your name;
  • your phone number / WhatsApp number;
  • your email address;
  • your delivery address in Bali;
  • your ring size;
  • your order details and payment confirmation;
  • the content of the messages you send us.

We do not ask for, and do not want, health, biometric or other specific personal data. The one exception is payment: if you pay by bank transfer, we see the details your bank shows on the transfer (such as the account holder's name and account number). Under the PDP Law this is specific personal data (personal financial data). We use it only to match your payment to your order, and keep it only as part of our accounting records.

If you are a child within the meaning of Indonesian law, we process your personal data only with the consent of your parent or legal guardian, as required by Article 25 of the PDP Law.

4. Who else sees your data

We share your data only with those who need it to deliver your order:

  • the courier company that delivers your ring in Bali — name, address, phone number;
  • payment providers — our bank, Wise or PayPal, depending on how you pay;
  • our website hosting and domain providers;
  • our email provider;
  • Meta, if you contact us through WhatsApp or Instagram — your message and profile data are processed by Meta under its own terms, and we cannot control that;
  • our accountant;
  • public authorities, where the law requires it.

We share with each of them only what they need, and we use providers that are bound to protect personal data.

5. Data leaving Indonesia

Some of our payment, hosting, email and messaging providers store or process data outside Indonesia — for example in the United States or the European Union.

In line with Article 56 of the PDP Law, we transfer personal data outside Indonesia only where the recipient's country provides a level of protection equal to or higher than the PDP Law, or where the provider is bound by adequate and binding safeguards (such as data processing terms with standard contractual clauses). If neither applies to a transfer, we will ask for your consent before making it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Invoices and accounting records: 10 years, as required by Indonesian tax law — a period we cannot shorten.
  • Order and delivery data: 5 years after the order is completed, then deleted — unless a dispute about the order is still open.
  • Messages that did not lead to an order: 12 months.

7. Your rights

Under the PDP Law you have the right to:

  • get information about who we are, why and on what legal basis we use your data, and how we are accountable for it;
  • complete, update or correct your data;
  • access your data and obtain a copy of it;
  • have us stop processing your data, and erase or destroy it;
  • withdraw any consent you have given us;
  • object to a decision based solely on automated processing;
  • have us postpone or restrict the processing of your data;
  • sue for and receive compensation for a breach in the processing of your data;
  • obtain your data in a structured, commonly used format, or have it sent to another controller.

Write to sila.thejewelry@gmail.com. We respond within the time limits set by the PDP Law — requests for access, correction, restriction of processing or withdrawal of consent are handled within 3 × 24 hours of receipt.

If you think we are handling your data badly, you can complain to the personal data protection authority of the Republic of Indonesia. Until that authority is formally established, complaints are handled by the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Kementerian Komunikasi dan Digital).

8. If your data is compromised

If the protection of your personal data fails, we will notify you and the competent authority in writing within 3 × 24 hours, as required by Article 46 of the PDP Law.

9. Cookies

Our website uses only the technical cookies needed for it to work. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not build profiles of visitors. This is why you do not see a cookie banner.

If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
