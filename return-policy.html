Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

1. Size exchange

Every ring is made by hand, and sizing is the one thing that is hard to get right from a screen. If your ring does not fit, we will exchange it for the same model in a different size.

How it works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram within 5 days of receiving your ring.
  2. Tell us your order details and the size you need.
  3. We confirm availability and give you the return address.
  4. Send the ring back by courier.
  5. Once we receive and inspect it, we dispatch the new size within 7 days.

Conditions

  • Exchange is for the same model in a different size only. We do not exchange for a different model, and we do not refund the price under this section.
  • The ring must be unworn and in perfect condition — no scratches, no scuffs, no signs of wear, no resizing or repair by anyone else — and returned in its original packaging.
  • Courier costs for the exchange are paid by you, in both directions.
  • The ring travels at your risk until it reaches us. Please use a trackable courier service.
  • If your size is not in stock, we make it. We will tell you how long that takes before you send your ring back, so you can decide.

If the returned ring shows signs of wear or damage, we will contact you, send it back to you, and the exchange will not go through.

2. Your statutory right to cancel (14 days)

Because we are a Polish company and your order is placed remotely, Polish and EU consumer law gives you a right that applies in addition to the exchange above.

If you are a consumer, you may cancel your order within 14 days of receiving the ring, without giving a reason.

  • Tell us by email or WhatsApp within those 14 days. A clear statement is enough.
  • Send the ring back within 14 days of telling us.
  • We refund the price and the standard delivery cost within 14 days of receiving the ring back, or of receiving proof that you sent it — whichever comes first. We refund using the same payment method you used.
  • You pay the cost of returning the ring.
  • You may examine the ring as you would in a shop. If you handle it beyond that and its value drops, we may reduce the refund accordingly.

3. If something is wrong with the ring

If your ring has a manufacturing fault, tell us as soon as you notice it. We are responsible for faults that were present when the ring was delivered to you and that appear within 2 years of delivery.

  • We pay all courier costs in this case.
  • Depending on the fault, we will repair the ring, replace it, reduce the price, or refund you.
  • Please send photographs with your first message — it usually saves a trip.

4. What is not a fault

Silver is a living material and handmade jewellery carries the marks of the hand that made it. The following are normal and are not covered:

  • Tarnishing and darkening of silver — this is natural oxidation and can be polished away.
  • Scratches, scuffs and loss of shine from everyday wear.
  • Bending or deformation caused by impact, pressure, or catching the ring on something.
  • Stones loosened or lost as a result of impact or pressure.
  • Damage from contact with cosmetics, perfume, hand sanitiser, chlorinated water, seawater, hot springs, household chemicals, or from sport and manual work.
  • Damage caused by resizing, repair, cleaning or plating carried out by anyone other than us.
  • Small variations in colour, texture, surface finish, and in the colour, shape or inclusions of natural stones. No two handmade rings are identical, and photographs on the site are indicative.
  • Normal wear over time, including thinning of the band on rings worn daily.
